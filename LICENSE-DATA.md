  CC BY 4.0. When you reuse them, cite: National Lung Screening Trial Research Team. Data from the National Lung Screening
  Trial (NLST) [Data set]. The Cancer Imaging Archive; 2013. doi:10.7937/TCIA.HMQ8-J677; and the Imaging Data Commons

  included. Their hashes are provided only so that a copy you obtain from the source can be verified.
